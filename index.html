SN-Launcher

A Google Nest Hub style launcher: it puts an old tablet on a wall and has it look like a smart display — the photograph frame, the big clock, the weather, the app drawer. Built for low-end wall-mounted hardware (1–4 GB RAM, so nothing you'd want to run on your phone has to do anything here).
