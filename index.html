SKLauncher (SimpleKioskLauncher)

A barebone launcher page for wall displays — it reads as the app the user sees: the photograph frame, the big clock, the date line — and below it a grid of the installed apps in the same look as the launcher sheet. Barebone on purpose: no quick settings, no suggestions, no search, no voice, no weather graphics. It is the page you set a tablet down on and just use.
